# Fridge Temperature Log Book

The fastest way to keep fridge, freezer, and hot-hold temperature records for health inspections — logging in about 10 seconds, offline, no account required.

### Frequently asked questions

**Where is my data stored?**
On your device only. Your readings, staff names, and business details never leave your phone except when you choose to share an exported PDF yourself.

**How do I restore my purchase after reinstalling or switching devices?**
Open the app, go to the Export tab, open the unlock screen, and tap "Restore purchases."

**How do I export a PDF?**
From the Export tab, tap "Export PDF." Logging is always free and unlimited — the one-time purchase unlocks full PDF export instead of the single-entry sample.
